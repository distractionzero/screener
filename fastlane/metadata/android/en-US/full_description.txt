Screener blocks spam and telemarketing calls using rules you control. It works alongside your existing phone app, keeps everything on your phone, and is made for Indian networks.

<b>Rules you control</b>

• Block or allow calls by phone number or by the caller name your network sends
• Use simple "starts with", "exactly" or "contains" rules, or full regular expressions
• Test a rule against a number before you save it
• Optionally block every caller that isn't in your contacts

<b>Made for India</b>

Ready-made rules that you can turn on or off:
• 140 series telemarketing numbers are blocked
• 160 series numbers used by banks, insurance and government are always allowed
• Common cloud telephony ranges used by marketing calls are blocked
• Calls labelled as spam, fraud or scam by Jio, Airtel, Vi or BSNL are blocked

<b>Works with your phone app</b>

Screener doesn't replace your dialer. It only takes Android's "Caller ID & spam" role, so Google Phone or any other phone app keeps working as usual. Calls from your saved contacts never reach Screener, so they always ring.

<b>See what was blocked</b>

• Activity shows every screened call, grouped by day, with the rule that matched
• Stats show how many calls were blocked over time
• Mark a number as spam from its call details
• Export and import your rules and activity as files

<b>Private and offline</b>

• No internet permission, so nothing can leave your phone
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account
• Your rules and call log are excluded from cloud backups

Requires Android 10 or newer.
